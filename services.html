Twin Cities Animal Rescue Services

Adoption Process Overview

Finding your perfect match should be an exciting and transparent journey. We’ve designed our adoption process to ensure the best possible fit for both you and your new companion.

  • Step 1: Submit an Application: Browse our available pets online and fill out a quick, no-obligation adoption application to tell us a little about your lifestyle and what you are looking for.
  • Step 2: The Meet-and-Greet: Our adoption coordinators will review your application and schedule a personal meeting with the animal and their foster family. This allows you to see the pet's true personality in a relaxed, realistic setting.
  • Step 3: Welcome Home: Once you know it's a match, we’ll finalize the adoption paperwork, provide a comprehensive medical history, and give you a transitional starter kit to ensure a smooth, successful integration into your family.

Foster Program Overview

Fostering is the absolute heartbeat of our rescue. Because we do not operate a centralized physical shelter, we rely on compassionate community members to open their homes to animals in transition. Whether you can foster for an emergency weekend or a few months, you are actively saving a life.

  • What We Provide: We supply everything you need. This includes all food, crates, collars, leashes, toys, and 100% of the medical care and veterinary expenses.
  • What You Provide: A safe indoor space, daily care, patience, and love while the animal decompresses and waits for their forever family.
  • Our Support System: You are never alone. Our foster team provides dedicated behavioral and medical support, ensuring you have the ongoing resources and guidance needed to help your foster pet thrive.

Volunteer Role Descriptions

We need passionate people with a variety of skills to keep our lifesaving mission moving forward. We offer flexible scheduling and training to fit your busy life.

  • Adoption Event Handlers: Be the bridge between our pets and the public. You’ll safely handle dogs or cats at our weekend community events, answer questions from potential adopters, and actively advocate for the animals in your care.
  • Freedom Ride Transporters: Help us move animals from overcrowded municipal shelters to our veterinary partners or safely deliver them to their new foster homes. A reliable vehicle, a valid driver's license, and a calm demeanor are all you need.
  • Digital Advocates & Storytellers: Help us capture their true spirits. We are always looking for volunteer photographers, videographers, and copywriters to capture the unique personalities of our adoptable pets and share our mission with the wider Twin Cities community online.
