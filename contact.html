Service Area

We proudly serve the entire Twin Cities metropolitan area and its surrounding suburbs. Because our rescue is entirely foster-based, our network of volunteers and temporary homes spans the region. We regularly coordinate adoptions, freedom transports, and community supply drives throughout the local metro footprint.

Office & Inquiry Hours

Please note: Twin Cities Animal Rescue does not operate a centralized public shelter facility. All pet meet-and-greets are scheduled by appointment only after an adoption application is approved. Our administrative and coordination teams are available to process applications and answer online inquiries during the following hours:

  • Monday – Friday: 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM
  • Saturday: 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM (Primarily dedicated to weekend adoption events)
  • Sunday: Closed to public inquiries (Active foster support and emergency medical intake only)
