Welcome to Twin Cities Animal Rescue

Our Mission

To rescue, rehabilitate, and rehome vulnerable animals across the metro area while empowering our community through education and compassionate action. We believe every animal deserves a safe landing, and we provide the medical care, behavioral support, and loving foster homes necessary to make those second chances a reality.

Services

  • Adoption Matching: We take the guesswork out of finding your new best friend. Our thorough behavioral assessments and personalized meet-and-greets ensure that every pet is paired with a home that fits their unique needs and personality.
  • Foster Care Network: We supply the food, medical care, and toys; you supply the love. Our robust foster program places animals in comfortable home environments so they can decompress and thrive while waiting for their forever families.
  • Community Pet Pantry: To help keep beloved pets in their homes where they belong, we offer temporary assistance with pet food and basic supplies to Twin Cities families facing sudden financial hardship.
  • Volunteer Opportunities: From coordinating adoption events and transporting animals to capturing photos for our website, we provide hands-on, flexible ways for local animal lovers to make a tangible, lifesaving impact.

About Us

Twin Cities Animal Rescue is a community-funded, foster-based nonprofit dedicated to serving the pets and people of our region. Unlike traditional shelters, we do not operate a centralized kennel facility. Instead, we rely entirely on a dedicated network of everyday heroes—our fosters, volunteers, and local veterinary partners—who open their homes and hearts to animals in crisis. We frequently step in when local municipal intake facilities run out of space, providing a crucial safety net for seniors, complex medical cases, and animals that have been continually overlooked. By focusing on transparent communication, high-quality medical rehabilitation, and steadfast community support, we are changing the landscape of animal welfare in the Twin Cities one rescue at a time.
