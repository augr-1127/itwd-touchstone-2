Welcome to Twin Cities Animal Rescue

Our Mission

To rescue, rehabilitate, and rehome vulnerable animals across the metro area while empowering our community through education and compassionate action. We believe every animal deserves a safe landing, and we provide the medical care, behavioral support, and loving foster homes necessary to make those second chances a reality.

Our Impact

  • 2,500+ Lives Saved: Over two thousand dogs and cats have been successfully matched with loving forever families since our founding in 2018.
  • 15,000+ Foster Nights: Our dedicated network of volunteer fosters provided more than 15,000 nights of safe, comfortable sleep in real homes last year alone.
  • 5,000 lbs of Food Distributed: Our Community Pet Pantry has supplied over two tons of pet food to local families facing financial hardship, keeping beloved pets in their homes.
  • 100% Medical Care Guarantee: Every single animal that enters our rescue receives a complete veterinary assessment, vaccinations, microchipping, and spay/neuter services before adoption.

How You Can Help

  • Adopt: Give a rescued pet the second chance they deserve. Browse our available animals and find a companion whose personality perfectly matches your lifestyle.
  • Foster: We provide the food, supplies, and medical care—you provide the love and a warm bed. Fostering saves two lives: the animal you take in, and the animal who gets to take their spot at a shelter.
  • Volunteer: We are entirely volunteer-driven. Whether you want to handle dogs at adoption events, transport cats to vet appointments, or help with social media from your couch, we need your skills.
  • Donate: Your financial gift directly funds emergency veterinary surgeries, preventative care, and daily enrichment. Become a monthly donor to provide a reliable safety net for animals in crisis.
