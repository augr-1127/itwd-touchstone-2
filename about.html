About Twin Cities Animal Rescue

Our Story

Every great rescue starts with a single act of compassion. Twin Cities Animal Rescue was founded in 2018 when a small group of local volunteers realized that too many adoptable dogs and cats in our community were slipping through the cracks of overwhelmed shelter systems. What began as a grassroots network of temporary foster homes has blossomed into a dedicated nonprofit organization serving the entire metro area. We believe that every animal deserves a second chance, a warm bed, and a family to call their own. Through tireless advocacy, community outreach, and an ever-growing family of fosters and volunteers, we are working every day to transform the lives of vulnerable pets. We aren't just saving animals; we are building a more compassionate Twin Cities. Whether you can offer an open home, a few hours of your time, or a financial gift, your involvement is the bridge between a rescue animal's difficult past and their bright future.

Our Values

  • Compassion in Action: We actively create a better future for animals. From middle-of-the-night emergency transports to daily enrichment activities, we put in the physical work required to heal both body and spirit.
  • Unshakable Trust: Transparency is at the core of everything we do. From our transparent financial practices to our honest, thorough behavioral assessments, we maintain the highest ethical standards for our animals, our adopters, and our donors.
  • Community Empowerment: Animal rescue is a collective effort. We believe in uplifting our neighbors by providing accessible pet food pantries, affordable training resources, and deeply rewarding, inclusive volunteer opportunities.
  • Relentless Optimism: Rescue work can be challenging, but our focus remains fixed on the joy of the happy ending. We view every single animal we intake as a beautiful, fulfilling life waiting to unfold.

Our Partners

Our mission thrives because of the incredible local businesses and organizations that stand shoulder-to-shoulder with us. We are profoundly grateful to North Star Veterinary Clinic, which provides critical emergency care and vital spay/neuter services for all our incoming rescues, ensuring they are healthy and ready for adoption. Mill City Pet Supply is our trusted retail partner, regularly hosting our bustling weekend adoption events and organizing quarterly supply drives so our foster families never have to pay out of pocket for food or toys. Furthermore, our unique collaboration with the Twin Cities Senior Center matches older, gentler pets with senior citizens, creating mutually beneficial companionship and freeing up crucial foster space. By supporting these generous community pillars, you are directly funding and facilitating our lifesaving work.
